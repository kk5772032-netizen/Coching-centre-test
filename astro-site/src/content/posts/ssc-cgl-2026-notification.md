The Staff Selection Commission usually releases the CGL notification in the first
week of March, with Tier 1 following roughly three months later. Below is what a
candidate starting now should plan around.

## Expected timeline

| Stage | Expected |
|---|---|
| Notification | First week of March 2026 |
| Application closes | Early April 2026 |
| Tier 1 | Late June 2026 |
| Tier 2 | September 2026 |

Treat these as planning estimates, not announced dates. We update this post the
day the official notification appears.

## Eligibility in short

A bachelor's degree in any discipline for most posts, with age limits varying by
post between 18–27, 20–30 and 18–30 years. Age relaxation applies as per the
usual category rules. Final-year students are eligible to apply provided they can
produce the degree before the document verification stage.

## What to do with the months before the notification

This is the most useful window in the whole cycle and most candidates waste it
waiting. Cover Advance Maths and English properly now — those are the two
sections that decide Tier 2, and they cannot be crammed in the six weeks before
the exam.
