Students preparing for CGL Tier 2 routinely try to cover every chapter in the
Advance Maths syllabus with equal weight. The papers do not reward that.

## What eight years of papers show

Going back through Tier 2 papers, the same clusters keep appearing:
Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration, Algebra, Height and Distance, and Data
Interpretation together account for the large majority of the Advance Maths
section. Within Geometry it narrows further — circles and triangles dominate.

## The practical consequence

Depth on twelve chapters beats shallow coverage of thirty. A student who can
solve any circle or triangle question in under ninety seconds is in a far better
position than one who has read every chapter once.

## How to build the speed

Speed comes from repetition on the same question types, not from learning more
shortcuts. In our Tier 2 batch students solve previous years' questions timed,
in class, with the analysis done immediately — because a paper you solved and
never reviewed teaches you very little.
