## Who this batch is for

Students beginning SSC CGL preparation from scratch, or repeaters who want to
rebuild their fundamentals rather than only practise papers. Roughly two-thirds
of the batch each year are graduates in their first serious attempt.

## How the year is structured

The first seven months cover the full Tier 1 and Tier 2 syllabus at a pace that
assumes no prior coaching. The next three months are entirely revision and test
practice. The final two months are mock tests under exam conditions with
individual paper analysis.

## What is included in the fee

Everything. Eleven printed modules, the complete test series, all class
handouts, and the daily doubt session. There is no separate charge for material
or tests at any point in the year.
