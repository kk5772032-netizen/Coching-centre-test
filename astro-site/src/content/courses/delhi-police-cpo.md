## The physical test is not an afterthought

More candidates fail PET/PST than the written paper. We have a tie-up with a
ground five minutes from the centre where students train four mornings a week
at no extra cost, with the measurement standards checked against the current
notification.

## Delhi-specific General Knowledge

The Delhi Police paper asks about Delhi — its administration, geography and
current affairs. We maintain a separate printed module for this that is updated
every year, because the standard SSC GK books do not cover it.
