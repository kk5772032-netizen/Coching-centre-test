## Live, not recorded

Classes are taught live at a fixed time with the camera and mic open for
questions. Recordings exist as a backup for when you miss a class, not as the
main format — students who only watch recordings almost never finish the course.

## Material reaches you

The same eleven printed modules used in the classroom batch are couriered to
your address in the first fortnight, included in the fee.
