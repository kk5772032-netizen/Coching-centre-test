## Designed around a six-day working week

Ten contact hours a weekend, plus recordings for weekday revision. The pace
assumes you can give roughly an hour on weekday nights — we say this plainly at
admission because students who cannot manage that tend to fall behind by month
four.

## Monthly mentor call

Because weekend students get less informal contact time, each one gets a
scheduled one-to-one call every month to review test scores and adjust the plan.
