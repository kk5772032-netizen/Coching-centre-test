Why students stay with us

Six things we actually do differently

No promises we cannot keep. These are the things you can verify on your demo day.

Batch size capped at 40

We stop admissions at 40 students per batch. The teacher knows every student's name and weak areas by the second month.

Faculty who cleared the exam

Three of our four core teachers have their own SSC selection. They teach the paper the way it is actually set, not from a generic textbook.

Doubt session every single day

One hour after every class, no appointment needed. If your doubt is not cleared that day, the teacher stays back.

Printed material, not PDFs

Eleven printed modules updated every year, handed over in the first week. Included in the fee — you never pay extra for books.

Weekly test, ranked the same evening

Every Sunday test is checked and ranked by Monday morning, with a printed analysis of which topics cost you marks.

One mentor for the whole year

Each student is assigned a mentor who tracks their test scores monthly and calls the parents once a quarter.

Before you ask

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay the fee in instalments?

Yes. Any batch above ₹20,000 can be paid in 2 or 3 instalments — first at admission, the rest at fixed dates we agree in writing. There is no interest or extra charge. We accept UPI, cash, cheque and bank transfer, and give a printed receipt every time.

How many students are there in one batch?

We cap every classroom batch at 40 students and stop admissions once it is full, even if people are still asking. If your preferred batch is full we will tell you honestly and put you on the next one rather than squeezing you in.

Do you give a free demo class?

Two free demo classes, in the actual running batch — not a special demo lecture. Parents are welcome to sit in. Book through the form above or just walk in between 10 AM and 6 PM with an ID proof.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund minus ₹1,000 administrative charge if you withdraw within 7 days of joining and return the printed material unused. Between 8 and 21 days, 50% is refunded. After 21 days no refund is possible, but the fee can be transferred to another batch once, or to a sibling.

Is study material included in the fee?

Yes — all eleven printed modules, the full test series and the class handouts are included. You will never be asked to buy a separate book or a separate test series from us. The only thing you pay extra for is a lost material set (₹350 per module).

Can I attend online if I live outside Delhi?

Yes. The Online Live Batch runs 6:30–9:00 PM with the same faculty as the classroom batch. Printed material is couriered to your address anywhere in India, recordings stay available for 12 months, and doubts are cleared on a dedicated WhatsApp group.

Do you help with PG or hostel nearby?

We do not run a hostel, but our office keeps a verified list of PGs in Mukherjee Nagar, Indira Vihar and Vijay Nagar in the ₹6,000–₹12,000 per month range, separately for boys and girls. Ask at the front desk and we will share contacts — we take no commission from any PG.

I am from Hindi medium. Will I manage the English paper?

Most of our students are from Hindi medium and clear it every year. Classes are taught bilingually, and the English module starts from basics with a daily 20-minute reading practice. Sunita ma'am also runs an extra 30-minute session twice a week for students who need it, at no additional cost.
